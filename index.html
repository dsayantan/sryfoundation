Sitaram Yechury · 1952 – 2024

A life of study, struggle and solidarity

The Sitaram Yechury Foundation carries forward the ideas, the scholarship and the unfinished work of a comrade who taught us that politics is ultimately about people.

His Life & Legacy 22 November Programme
The Foundation

“Not merely to remember him, but to continue the journey.”

Sitaram Yechury brought together Marxist theory and the living realities of people’s struggles, always insisting on the need for a ‘concrete analysis of concrete conditions’. Student leader, scholar, parliamentarian and General Secretary of the CPI(M), he was respected across the political spectrum for his integrity, erudition and warmth.

The Foundation is dedicated to keeping that spirit alive — through study and debate, through the encouragement of young scholars, and through the preservation of his writings, speeches and photographs for a new generation.

Milestones

Elected to the Polit Bureau at the 14th Congress

Twelve years in the Rajya Sabha from West Bengal
